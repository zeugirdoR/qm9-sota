## Interpretation

The gentle scheduled Droplet signal survived the transition from smoke mode to the full 110k training split.

This is not a SOTA claim. It is evidence that A1b is a valid candidate regularizer for the TinyRadialMPNN baseline.

A2 learnable offsets remain on hold because they were effectively neutral relative to A1b.

## Decision

Promote A1b to validated full-split TinyRadialMPNN regularizer.
